# Literature reviews for the closed-loop experiments

These are reviews of published work, written to plan experiments on this
branch. Each review says what the literature found, what our store already
holds that bears on it, and what follows for us.
- **They are reading material, not knowledge entries.** The store's entries
  in `librarian_agent/kb/entries/` stay the authority.
- **A number gets into a plan only one way.** The librarian ingests it, one
  source at a time, as a graded entry: a paper's number is E3 at best.

**Not yet searched: the third experiment.** That is the experiment and the
simulation sharing results in real time to optimise each other's parameters.
It is the ultimate goal, and which experiment it uses is not yet chosen. It
gets its own review once that choice is made.

**How the reviews were checked.**
- Every cited DOI was resolved against Crossref, and every arXiv id against
  arXiv, on the date of writing.
- Each reference carries its read level: full text, abstract only, or a
  search excerpt.
- Papers that could not be confirmed are listed separately at the end of each
  review.
