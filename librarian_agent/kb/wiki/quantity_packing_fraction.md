## Claims

### [dipolar_active_brownian_spheres_dipole_sum_without_ewald_at_low_density](../entries/dipolar_active_brownian_spheres_dipole_sum_without_ewald_at_low_density.json)

For three-dimensional dipolar active Brownian spheres at low density (volume fractions 0.010 and 0.08), Kelidou et al. summed the dipole-dipole interaction without an Ewald correction wherever the largest dipolar energy at half the box length stayed below one thousandth of kT, and a few LAMMPS runs with full Ewald and k-space corrections gave consistent structures and identical pair distribution functions; their denser, strongly coupled runs were done with full Ewald summation instead.

Identifiers: none

How it is known: from a document, a reading off the instrument, or another project's record; published literature (E3 · literature:src_kelidou_2024_dipolar_active_brownian).

Conditions, verbatim:

> A METHOD CHOICE REPORTED AS MATTERING, validated inside the paper. The criterion held for every coupling at volume fraction 0.010 (box length 76.02 sigma with 8,788 particles) and for lambda up to 6.25 at volume fraction 0.08; the runs at 0.08 with lambda from 9 to 12.25 used LAMMPS with Ewald. A larger system (19,652 particles) gave the same pair distribution at lambda = 16. The energy bound is in kT and is kept in words.

### [repulsive_disks_in_active_noise_structure_set_by_lambda_and_va_tau](../entries/repulsive_disks_in_active_noise_structure_set_by_lambda_and_va_tau.json)

In the same two-dimensional active-noise model, at area fractions 0.1 and 0.4, the static structure factor depends on the noise only through the correlation length lambda_a and the second length va*tau_a: changing va and tau_a with their product held fixed leaves the structure factor unchanged and only makes the particles move faster or slower.

Identifiers: none

How it is known: from a document, a reading off the instrument, or another project's record; published literature (E3 · literature:src_frechette_2025_active_noise_clustering).

Conditions, verbatim:

> A MODEL RESULT. Repulsive WCA disks (cutoff 2^(1/6) sigma), overdamped, each displaced by the potential force plus the local value of a Gaussian noise field correlated over lambda_a in space and tau_a in time with magnitude va, standing in for an active fluid; no separate thermal noise; HOOMD-blue (version not stated) with the authors' noise generator; box 200 x 200 and grid spacing 0.5 in reduced units (sigma, epsilon, t0 = gamma sigma^2/epsilon), time step 1e-4, 250 time units, 50 independent runs. The authors call the model highly simplified and not expected to be quantitatively accurate. The noise parameters are in reduced units the registry cannot express and so appear here in words only. Shown at two-dimensional area fractions 0.1 and 0.4 (the Fig. 6 insets, with further cases in the Supplemental Information). For a single tracer the paper also reports that in the limit of infinite correlation length the particle becomes an active Ornstein-Uhlenbeck particle with persistence time tau_a and diffusivity va^2 tau_a.

### [shear_stress_fluctuation_shape_marks_the_glass_fluid_transition](../entries/shear_stress_fluctuation_shape_marks_the_glass_fluid_transition.json)

In the same model at volume fraction 0.66, the skewness and excess kurtosis of the distribution of shear-stress magnitudes change more sharply across the glass-fluid transition than the mean stress does: in the fluid the magnitude is effectively half-normal (skewness about 0.995, excess kurtosis about 0.869), in the glass the skewness is near zero and the excess kurtosis negative, and a contour at 0.5 in either marks the transition.

Identifiers: none

How it is known: from a document, a reading off the instrument, or another project's record; published literature (E3 · literature:src_shee_2025_active_dopants_rheology).

Conditions, verbatim:

> A MODEL RESULT. Dry three-dimensional mixture of soft repulsive spheres (a harmonic repulsion approximated by a Morse form), half small and half large (radius ratio sqrt(2)) in both the active and the passive population; active particles are overdamped active Brownian particles, passive ones have no propulsion; kT = 1e-4, Dr = 3 Dt/a0^2 with a0 the small diameter; steady shear with Lees-Edwards boundaries; Euler-Maruyama at time step 1e-2 in HOOMD-blue (version not stated); total strain 10 with the first 5 discarded; no hydrodynamics, no walls, no friction. Peclet number v0/(a0 Dr), which is peclet_number_steric with the small diameter as the length. Shear rates in model units from 1e-6 to 1e-2. Volume fractions (three dimensions) from 0.60 to 0.72. Shown at volume fraction 0.66 and the lowest shear rate, 1e-6 in model units. A method the paper offers as an additional and probably better criterion than the mean stress; the half-normal values follow from uncorrelated collisions in a flowing fluid.
