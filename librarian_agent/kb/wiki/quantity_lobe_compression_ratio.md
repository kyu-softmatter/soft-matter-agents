## Claims

### [depletion_driven_lobed_diamond_forms_at_or_below_the_kissing_size_ratio](../entries/depletion_driven_lobed_diamond_forms_at_or_below_the_kissing_size_ratio.json)

Experimentally, depletion-driven cubic diamond from tetrahedrally lobed patchy particles forms at size ratios below or near the kissing geometry -- patches retracted, lobes fully interlocked with six contacts -- whereas for DNA-mediated patch-patch binding the simulations the authors cite find diamond only above the kissing geometry; they attribute the difference to depletion favouring the higher coordination and larger excluded-volume overlap of the fully interlocked state.

How it is known: from a document, a reading off the instrument, or another project's record; published literature (E3 · literature:src_kim_2026_depletion_colloidal_diamond).

Conditions, verbatim:

> AN EXPERIMENTAL OBSERVATION with a comparison to other work. The kissing geometry is the size ratio at which neighbouring patches just touch while the lobes stay fully interlocked; it depends on the compression ratio and is drawn only in Fig. 5, so no numerical boundary is entered. The contrast with DNA binding rests on the authors' reference 10 (Gales et al. 2025) and a re-analysis of He et al. 2020 in their SI; see dna_patchy_tetrahedral_clusters_crystallise_diamond_at_compression_0_63_to_0_78 for that system.

### [lobed_patchy_diamond_nucleation_out_of_reach_stability_mapped_by_melting](../entries/lobed_patchy_diamond_nucleation_out_of_reach_stability_mapped_by_melting.json)

Kim et al. found that unbiased Langevin simulations of their tetrahedrally lobed patchy particles started from a disordered gas gave spontaneous cubic diamond only for some parameter sets, nucleation and growth not being seen within reachable simulation times over much of the compression-ratio and size-ratio space, and mapped the stability region instead by melting runs started from the crystal.

How it is known: from a document, a reading off the instrument, or another project's record; published literature (E3 · literature:src_kim_2026_depletion_colloidal_diamond).

Conditions, verbatim:

> A MODEL RESULT. Tetrahedrally lobed patchy particles modelled as five spheres (four lobes and one central sphere for the patches) with an Alexander-de Gennes brush repulsion plus Asakura-Oosawa depletion (parameters in the SI, not in the file); Langevin dynamics in HOOMD-blue v2.9.7. Melting runs: 600 particles started in cubic diamond, 1e8 steps at time step 0.0001, stability judged by the fraction still diamond by common-neighbour analysis in OVITO; parameter sets outside the region melted completely. A melting run bounds where the crystal is stable, not where it can nucleate, so a plan reading this should not expect spontaneous diamond everywhere inside the region.
