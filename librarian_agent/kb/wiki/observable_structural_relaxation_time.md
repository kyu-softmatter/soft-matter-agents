## What it is

From the managers' registry, contracts/observables.json:

> The time a two-dimensional suspension of repulsive particles takes to reach its ordered steady state from a random initial configuration, read off the bond-orientational order parameter psi6. It is a property of the relaxation from a stated initial condition and not of the steady state it reaches.

- units: ["s"]
- estimator: Compute psi6 per particle from its neighbours by Voronoi or a stated cutoff, average over particles to a single psi6(t), and report the time at which psi6(t) first reaches a DECLARED FRACTION of its plateau value, fitted over a declared lag window. The plateau fraction and the fit window are both conditions the plan must carry.
