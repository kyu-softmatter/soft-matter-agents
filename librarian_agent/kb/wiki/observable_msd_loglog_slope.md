## What it is

From the managers' registry, contracts/observables.json:

> The local slope of log(MSD) against log(lag) over a declared lag range. Two for ballistic motion, one for diffusive, and the value between them is what a crossover looks like.

- units: ["1"]
- estimator: Least-squares slope of log(MSD) on log(lag) over a DECLARED lag range. Short-time and long-time slopes are this one quantity at two windows, not two quantities. Distinguish in the plan whether MSD/(2*d*t) or the local derivative d(MSD)/dt/(2*d) underlies the curve being differentiated: the two differ by exactly the amount this slope is measuring.
