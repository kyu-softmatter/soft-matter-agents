## Claims

### [dipolar_active_brownian_spheres_dipole_sum_without_ewald_at_low_density](../entries/dipolar_active_brownian_spheres_dipole_sum_without_ewald_at_low_density.json)

For three-dimensional dipolar active Brownian spheres at low density (volume fractions 0.010 and 0.08), Kelidou et al. summed the dipole-dipole interaction without an Ewald correction wherever the largest dipolar energy at half the box length stayed below one thousandth of kT, and a few LAMMPS runs with full Ewald and k-space corrections gave consistent structures and identical pair distribution functions; their denser, strongly coupled runs were done with full Ewald summation instead.

Identifiers: none

How it is known: from a document, a reading off the instrument, or another project's record; published literature (E3 · literature:src_kelidou_2024_dipolar_active_brownian).

Conditions, verbatim:

> A METHOD CHOICE REPORTED AS MATTERING, validated inside the paper. The criterion held for every coupling at volume fraction 0.010 (box length 76.02 sigma with 8,788 particles) and for lambda up to 6.25 at volume fraction 0.08; the runs at 0.08 with lambda from 9 to 12.25 used LAMMPS with Ewald. A larger system (19,652 particles) gave the same pair distribution at lambda = 16. The energy bound is in kT and is kept in words.
