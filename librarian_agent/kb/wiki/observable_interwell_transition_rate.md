## What it is

From the managers' registry, contracts/observables.json:

> The number of crossings from one named well of a multi-well optical potential to another, per unit time spent in the origin well. Reported per ordered pair. Where exactly two wells exist it is the reciprocal of well_residence_time for that pair.

- units: ["1/s", "Hz"]
- estimator: Count milestoning transitions out of the origin well over the record and divide by the total time assigned to the ORIGIN well, not by the record length. Dividing by the record length reports a rate diluted by however long the particle sat in the other well, which makes the number depend on the barrier it is not about.
