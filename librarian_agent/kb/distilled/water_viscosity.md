# Water viscosity near room temperature

**Claim** Dynamic viscosity of pure water near 293 K is of order 0.001 Pa*s.

**Under what conditions** Pure water, roughly 288 to 298 K, atmospheric
pressure.

**What it does not cover** The buffer actually in the sample chamber, and any
glycerol mixture. Both raise the viscosity by amounts that matter.

**The limit worth remembering** The value moves by about two percent per kelvin.
In explore mode that is invisible — it does not change the decade. In confirm
mode it is the difference between agreeing and disagreeing with a model, which
is why a temperature has to travel with the number rather than beside it.
