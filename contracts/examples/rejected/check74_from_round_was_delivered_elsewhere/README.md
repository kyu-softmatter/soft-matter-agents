# check 74 — standing on a round handed to someone else

The round exists in this group and was delivered into `microscope_agent/inbox/`.
The goal citing it sits elsewhere, so it is standing on an envelope addressed
to another agent.

§7.1 rule 8 puts the delivery in the receiving agent's tree precisely so that
taking a round means having been handed one. A card citing a round delivered
elsewhere has helped itself to it.

**The round and the delivery are inside the group on purpose.** An earlier
version of this fixture cited the real `thr-tracer-diffusivity-001:r1`, and
under `--expect-fail` the repository's own `bridge/threads/` is not loaded --
so it failed as *no such round exists*, one branch short of what it claims to
test, while the group still counted as rejected. A group names its check and
not its branch.
